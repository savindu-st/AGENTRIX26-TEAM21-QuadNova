# Official Service Rules: Passport Application & Urgent One-Day Service

**Service Name**: Passport Application
**Service ID**: passport_application
**Category**: Travel & Passports
**Office Type**: Department of Immigration and Emigration (Head Office in Battaramulla or Regional Branches in Kandy, Matara, Vavuniya, Kurunegala)
**Urgent One-Day Fee**: 20,000 LKR
**Standard Service Fee**: 10,000 LKR (takes 30 days)

## Description
Sri Lankan citizens can apply for a passport under standard or urgent one-day service.

## Urgent One-Day Walk-in Protocol
For one-day service, citizens can walk into the Head Office (Battaramulla) or authorized regional branches (Matara, Kandy, Kurunegala). Office opens at 8:30 AM. Tokens are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Required Documents
1. **Application Form (Form K-35A)**: Completed form.
2. **Current NIC**: Original and a photocopy.
3. **Original Birth Certificate**: Must be submitted along with a copy.
4. ** GN Certified Photos**: Three (3) colored photographs according to specifications.
