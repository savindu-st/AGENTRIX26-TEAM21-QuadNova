# Official Service Rules: Social Services Navigation (Elderly Assistance)

**Service Name**: Elderly Monthly Assistance
**Service ID**: elderly_assistance
**Category**: Social Services & Public Assistance
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat (Social Services Branch)
**Official Fee**: Free of charge

## Description
Submission of monthly financial assistance forms for senior citizens.

## Office Navigation (Counter Details)
At the Divisional Secretariat, citizens submitting elderly assistance applications must go to **Room 5** (Social Services Branch) or **Counter 7** (Public Aid Desk).
Our crowd reports verify that bringing a photocopy of the mother's NIC and GN residency confirmation helps bypass delay.
