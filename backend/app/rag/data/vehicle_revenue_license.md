# Official Service Rules: Vehicle Revenue License Renewal (Transport)

**Service Name**: Vehicle Revenue License Renewal
**Service ID**: revenue_license
**Category**: Transport & Licensing
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat (Motor Traffic Department branch) or Online (e-Revenue License system)
**Official Fee**: Varies by vehicle class and weight.

## Description
Annual renewal of vehicle revenue license. Can be done online or physically at the Divisional Secretariat.

## Missing Eco-Test / Emission Test Online Bypass
If the online system fails or reports a "missing eco-test" (emission certificate) despite having one, the citizen must renew the license **physically** at the Divisional Secretariat.

## Required Documents (Physical Bypass)
1. **Vehicle Registration Document (CR)**: Original Certificate of Registration (Logbook).
2. **Valid Emission Certificate (Eco-Test)**: Hardcopy from authorized testing center (DriveGreen or Laugfs Eco Sri).
3. **Valid Insurance Policy Certificate**: Minimum third-party cover.

## Form Details
- **Form Title**: Form MTA-39
- **Form Act**: MOTOR TRAFFIC ACT, NO. 14 OF 1951
- **Form Subtitle**: Application for Vehicle Revenue License Renewal
- **Form Field Label**: 4. Emission Test Status:
- **Form Field Value**: Passed / Exempt
- **Form Description Label**: 5. Revenue license remarks & details:
- **Form Default Description**: Renewal of standard vehicle revenue license
