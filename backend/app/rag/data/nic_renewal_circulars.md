# District Circular Notes: Matara Divisional Secretariat

**Service Name**: National Identity Card (NIC) Renewal
**Service ID**: nic_renewal
**Office Name**: Matara Divisional Secretariat
**District**: Matara
**District Specific Fee**: 550 LKR (includes 50 LKR local administrative surcharge)
**Officer Room**: Room 12 (NIC Division)
**Processing Hours**: Mondays and Wednesdays only, from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

## Local Office Requirements
1. **Residence Certificate**: Due to district policy, citizens in Matara must submit a Residence Certificate issued by the Grama Niladhari within the last 3 months.
2. **Pre-verification**: Applications must be signed and stamped by the Divisional Secretary before submission at Counter 3.
