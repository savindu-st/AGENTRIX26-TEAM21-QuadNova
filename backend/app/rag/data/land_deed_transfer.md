# Official Service Rules: Land Deed Title Transfer (Property)

**Service Name**: Land Deed Title Transfer
**Service ID**: land_deed_transfer
**Category**: Property & Land Registry
**Office Type**: Land Registry (Divisional Secretariat region)
**Official Fee**: Stamp duty (varies based on property valuation) + Registration fee (1,000 LKR).

## Description
Transfer of land ownership between family members or buyers.

## Step-by-Step Title Transfer Process
1. **Title Search**: Must perform a title search at the local Land Registry to verify ownership and check for encumbrances.
2. **Drafting of Deed**: A licensed Notary Public must draft the transfer deed.
3. **Valuation & Stamp Duty**: Pay stamp duty at a state bank based on the land value.
4. **Execution**: The transferor (e.g. grandmother) and transferee (grandson) must sign the deed before the Notary Public and two witnesses.
5. **Registration**: Submit the executed deed to the Land Registry for registration and update of the title index.
