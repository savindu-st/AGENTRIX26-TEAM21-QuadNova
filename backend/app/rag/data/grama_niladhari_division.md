# Official Service Rules: Grama Niladhari Division Search (Interactive Triage)

**Service Name**: Find Grama Niladhari Division
**Service ID**: gn_division_lookup
**Category**: Regional Administration
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat region
**Official Fee**: Free of charge

## Description
Guidelines on how to locate or verify your local Grama Niladhari (GN) division name or number.

## GN Division Lookup Guidance
If a citizen does not know their Grama Niladhari (GN) division:
1. **Interactive Resolution**: Request the citizen's exact village name, municipal ward, or street name.
2. **GN Lookup Map**: Use the regional gazette or Divisional Secretariat's village-to-division mapping directory to find the division code.
3. **GN Office**: The local Divisional Secretariat has an administrative officer at the **GN Administration Desk (Room 2)** who can verify the division name based on the village address.
