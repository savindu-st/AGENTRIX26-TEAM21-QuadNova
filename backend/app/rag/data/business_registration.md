# Official Service Rules: Local Business Registration (Commerce)

**Service Name**: Local Business Registration
**Service ID**: business_registration
**Category**: Commerce & Trade
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat (Business Registration branch)
**Official Fee**: 2,500 LKR (Sole Proprietorship)

## Description
Registration of a sole proprietorship, partnership, or business name. Small home-based businesses (like software consulting) must register at the **Divisional Secretariat** of their business address, not at the Pradeshiya Sabha (which only handles municipal trade licenses/rates).

## Required Documents
1. **Business Registration Form (Form A)**: Duly filled application.
2. **GN Certificate**: Resident/location approval signed by the Grama Niladhari.
3. **Deed / Lease Agreement**: Proof of the home/office property ownership or lease.
4. **NIC Copy**: Owner's identity card.
