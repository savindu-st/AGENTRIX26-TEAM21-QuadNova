# Official Service Rules: National Identity Card (NIC) Renewal

**Service Name**: National Identity Card (NIC) Renewal
**Service ID**: nic_renewal
**Category**: Personal Identification
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat (Department of Registration of Persons branch)
**Official Fee**: 500 LKR
**Official Processing Time**: 14 Days

## Description
This service allows Sri Lankan citizens to renew their National Identity Card (NIC) due to expiration, damage, or changes in personal details.

## Required Official Documents
1. **Application Form (Annexure 1)**: Duly filled application form certified by the Grama Niladhari.
2. **Current/Old NIC**: The original card to be handed over (or a police report if lost).
3. **Original Birth Certificate**: Must be submitted along with a copy.
4. **Photographs**: Three (3) color photographs of size 1.3" x 1.8", certified on the reverse side by the Grama Niladhari.
5. **Marriage Certificate**: Required only if name has changed due to marriage (original and photocopy).
