# Official Service Rules: Permit to Cut a Protected Tree (Agriculture)

**Service Name**: Permit to Cut a Protected Tree
**Service ID**: tree_cutting_permit
**Category**: Agriculture & Environment
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat (Agrarian Services / Environment branch)
**Official Fee**: 100 LKR

## Description
Under Sri Lankan law, certain trees (including Jack/Kos, Breadfruit/Del, and Palmyrah) are protected. Citizens must obtain a formal permit before cutting them down, even on private property.

## Required Documents
1. **Application Form**: Available at the Grama Niladhari (GN) office.
2. **Proof of Ownership**: Original land deed and a certified copy.
3. **GN Recommendation**: GN must inspect the tree (checking if it risks buildings like roofs) and sign the recommendation.
4. **Photographs**: Visual proof of the tree's position relative to the house/roof.
