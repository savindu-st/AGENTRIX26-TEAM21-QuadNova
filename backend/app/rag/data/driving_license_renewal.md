# Official Service Rules: Driving License Renewal (Transport)

**Service Name**: Driving License Renewal
**Service ID**: driving_license_renewal
**Category**: Transport & Licensing
**Office Type**: Divisional Secretariat or Department of Motor Traffic (DMT)
**Official Fee**: 2,500 LKR
**Official Processing Time**: 1 Day

## Description
This service allows Sri Lankan citizens to renew their driving license.

## Required Documents
1. **Application Form (Form DL-1)**: Duly filled application form.
2. **Current Driving License**: The original to be renewed.
3. **Medical Fitness Certificate**: Issued by the National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI).
4. **NIC Copy**: Owner's identity card copy.

## Form Details
- **Form Title**: Form DL-1
- **Form Act**: MOTOR TRAFFIC ACT, NO. 14 OF 1951
- **Form Subtitle**: Application for the Renewal / Issue of Driving License
- **Form Field Label**: 4. Driving Vehicle Class:
- **Form Field Value**: Class B (Light Cars & Dual Purpose Vehicles)
- **Form Description Label**: 5. License validity renewal justifications:
- **Form Default Description**: Renewal of standard vehicle driving license
